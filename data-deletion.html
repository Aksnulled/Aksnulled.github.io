User Data Deletion Instructions

Our games, including Touchdown Rush, do not require an account and do not store personal information such as your name or email on our servers. Gameplay progress is saved locally on your device.

Delete local data

Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your device settings (Android: Settings › Apps › Touchdown Rush › Storage › Clear data).

Request deletion of analytics data

Send an email to axel.webinar@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the name of the game. If possible, include your device's advertising ID (Android: Settings › Google › Ads). We will process your request and forward it to our analytics and advertising partners within 30 days.

Facebook data

If you have interacted with our games through Facebook, you can also remove the app from your Facebook account: Settings & privacy › Settings › Apps and websites, then remove the app.
