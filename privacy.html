Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unplay ("we", "us") handles information when you play our mobile games, including Touchdown Rush (the "Games").

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email or phone number in the Games. When you play, the Games and the third-party services listed below may automatically collect technical information such as: device model and operating system, advertising identifier (GAID / IDFA) where permitted, IP address, approximate country, app version, and gameplay events (for example level started, level completed, session length).

How we use it

  • To operate and improve the Games and fix bugs;
  • To measure gameplay statistics and retention;
  • To measure the performance of our advertising campaigns (attribution);
  • Where applicable, to show advertising.

Third-party services

The Games use third-party SDKs that process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Voodoo (publishing partner) – voodoo.io/privacy
  • GameAnalytics – gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Meta / Facebook SDK – facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Adjust – adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy
  • Unity – unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Your choices and rights

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy › Tracking). If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. To exercise these rights, see our data deletion instructions or contact us.

Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Retention

Analytics data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, according to each provider's retention policy.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
